Overview

Helping sellers list, price, and manage inventory with confidence

At SeatGeek I designed for the seller team, focusing on Smart Pricing — improving adoption and conversion so sellers can price tickets better and move inventory at scale. This case study walks through the problem space, research, design process, and iterations that shaped the experience.
