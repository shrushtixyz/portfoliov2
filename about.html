Shrushti Chavan, designer from Jersey City > San Francisco

I aspire to build things that really impact and change lives. I found that more than visual design, I'm drawn to systems, fast iteration, and mapping end-to-end thinking. I am dedicated to delivering intuitive, scalable experiences and solving complex systems problems, working on numerous products from AI tooling and live marketplaces to mobile and desktop apps.

I like tackling product and design challenges, attaching logic and feasibility to ship from 0>1. I hope to continue to build cool shit.

I believe that everything is designed. Design can change just about everything about a product and it is a basis for how all our systems work. If a system does not work, it is not designed well. I believe I can understand the deeper system and how it works in order to design a better one. I find this diagnosis/problem solving skill in so many aspects of my life. I also write about it.

Outside of work and school I like running sidequests. Hosting events, hiking, trying new food, cooking.

I love meeting new people so please reach out or find me on X and Linkedin.
